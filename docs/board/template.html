The evening feeds the next morning, so the device always drills words you met yourself. It works without a connection all day and goes online once, in the evening, through your phone.

Pronunciation, in three layers

Written and heard, both. The written line works in silence on a train. Sound comes from clips prepared in advance. The microphone lets you hear yourself next to the model. The device cannot judge your pronunciation by itself, and says so.

The same four words in three notations. 箸, 橋 and 端 are all はし; only the pitch tells them apart.
